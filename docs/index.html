Engineered for Mathematicians & Researchers

Seamlessly dictate complex scientific math into any application without taking your hands off the keyboard.

⚡

Auto-Paste at Cursor

Types wherever your cursor is blinking. Dictate equations directly into Overleaf, Notion, Google Docs, VS Code, Obsidian, or Slack.

⌨️

Global Push-to-Talk Hotkey

Hit ⌘ + Shift + L anywhere across macOS. Speak your formula in regular English, press again, and your compiled LaTeX appears instantly.

🧠

Mathematical Context Understanding

Understands tensors, Greek letters, bounds, fractions, operator hats, bra-kets, and differentials without forcing you to memorize macro names like "backslash".

🪟

Dual Window & Menu Bar App

Lives discreetly in your macOS top menu bar as ∫, and offers a full standalone window with a built-in Permissions Setup & Diagnostics dashboard.

🚀

Sub-300ms Inference

Powered by Groq's high-throughput LPU running Whisper Large v3 and Llama 3.1 8B Instant, plus support for OpenAI GPT-4o-mini and Google Gemini.

🔒

Private & Zero Telemetry

Your audio and formulas are never tracked or saved to third-party telemetry servers. Use your own free API keys or offline fallback engine.

Three Steps to Effortless LaTeX

From speech to formatted publication-ready LaTeX in under a second.

01

Press ⌘+Shift+L

Place your cursor in Overleaf, Notion, Google Docs, or VS Code, and press the global Push-to-Talk hotkey.

02

Speak in Plain English

Say "The integral from zero to infinity of x squared times e to the minus x dx equals two" or any advanced formula.

03

Watch It Type

WhisperTeX transcribes, formats, wraps with your chosen delimiter ($$, $, or \[\dots\]), and simulates Cmd+V directly at your cursor.
